Hey you,

I know I haven't always been the person you deserved me to be, and I'm really sorry for the moments where my actions hurt you. I don't expect one long message to fix everything. I just want you to know that I understand why you're hurt.

You are genuinely important to me. I love the little things about you, the way you make ordinary conversations feel special, and all the memories we've made together. Having you in my life means more to me than I probably say enough.

I don't want you to lower your expectations of me just because I've messed up. You're allowed to expect effort, care, consistency and the little things. Instead of asking you to expect less, I want to become someone who can actually meet those expectations.

So this isn't me asking you to instantly forgive me or pretend everything is normal. Take whatever time you need. I just hope that, with time, my actions can show you how much you matter to me.

Thank you for being you. ❤️

— from someone who really, really cares about you
